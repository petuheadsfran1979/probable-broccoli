### Step 3: Accept the License Agreement


As the installation wizard opens, you will be prompted to read and accept Quicken’s License Agreement. It’s essential to review this agreement to understand what you’re agreeing to, even if it seems lengthy. After reading, click on the “I Agree” button to proceed.
